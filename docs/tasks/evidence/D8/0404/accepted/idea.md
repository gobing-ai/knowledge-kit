# Morning chamber — dark pastoral electronic

Operator's idea, captured from the lyria studio project `proj-1795030763741-2n8j8s`
(2026-11-20): a 2:30 chamber piece for daily morning listening crossed with an electronic track.

**Technical direction**

- 150 BPM, D minor, 4/4
- Low male baritone lead vocal
- Down-tuned rhythm guitars, distorted electronic bass
- Dark, aggressive production; wide choruses, narrow intimate verses

**Avoid**: falsetto, orchestral strings, long ambient introduction.

**Theme & objects**: 从空谷幽兰、林间鸟语中醒来，楼下溪水潺潺一派清幽的田园牧歌景象
(wake among valley orchids and birdsong, a stream below, a clear pastoral scene).

**Structure as sketched**

- 0:00–0:10 Intro — filtered electronic percussion, distant guitar noise
- 0:10–0:38 Verse 1 — low baritone vocal, restrained palm-muted guitars
- 0:54–1:22 Chorus — full-width guitars, heavy drums, stacked vocal harmonies

**Chosen theme preset**: `synthwave`.
