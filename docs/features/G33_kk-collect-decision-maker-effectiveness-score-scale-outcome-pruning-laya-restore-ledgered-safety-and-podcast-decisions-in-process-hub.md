# G33: kk-collect decision-maker effectiveness: score scale, outcome pruning, laya restore, ledgered safety and podcast decisions, in-process hub

## Goal

Make kk's decision-maker usage evidence-led (task 0317 audit, 2026-10-19). Every maker or prompt choice is justified by a labels × selections outcome join, not by accept rate or confidence. Model scores share one scale with the heuristic. Dimensions with no predictive signal stop costing a model call. Quality is back on the probability-native laya maker. The daily safety verdict and the podcast outline pick become ledgered catalog decisions with a fail-safe fallback. Batch labelling runs through one warm in-process decision hub instead of 8+ `spur decision run` subprocesses per item.

## Scope

- In: score-scale pinning between the model rubric and the heuristic; the `decision-signal-report` outcome join; the per-dimension maker A/B and the drop decision for graded dimensions in the default label pass; the `kk-item-quality: laya-local` restore once spur ships the laya worker; the `kk-safety-verdict` catalog decision and the `kind: decide` verdict step in `kk-daily-safety`; the `kk-podcast-outline` auto-profile binding in `kk-solo-podcast`; the `@gobing-ai/ts-ai-decision` in-process hub with a latched subprocess fallback.
- Out: wiring any graded model score into selection ranking (no dimension shows signal); `llm-agent` A/B (waits on ts-libs 0115); the TypeSafe driver's in-process packaging (`@opentelemetry/api` peer, an upstream ts-infra fix).
