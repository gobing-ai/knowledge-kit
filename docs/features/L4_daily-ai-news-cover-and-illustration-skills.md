# L4: Daily AI news cover and illustration skills

## Goal

Give daily AI news a consistent, source-grounded visual identity and make cover and illustration direction reusable in scheduled and standalone work.

## Scope

### In scope

- Own daily cover visual direction and daily illustration direction in two dedicated kk skills.
- Cover podcast and social cover outputs, scheduled Xiaohongshu card visuals, and optional inline article figures backed by reviewed content and supported delivery.
- Reuse image-gen, existing presets, pinned defaults, typography, and handoff artifacts. Limit plugin enhancements to demonstrated provider-neutral gaps.

### Out of scope

- Generic image workflows, provider experiments, editorial title and claim ownership, and publish policy.
